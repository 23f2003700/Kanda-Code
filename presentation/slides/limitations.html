Limitations

What our model does not claim

Composite, not Lasalgaon

Maharashtra price runs ≈10% below Lasalgaon, so Lasalgaon gains would be higher: Nasik gives +Rs 11,510 and +Rs 13,377/t in 2023–24.

H1 and H4 are associations

H1 weakens with other arrival measures (p 0.06–0.08) and vanishes with last month’s price. H4 (rain) is fragile: drop 2005, 2018 or 2019 and p > 0.05.

Forecasts biased low

Test MAPE 29.5%, bias −17%. Value comes from the seasonal shape; a fixed Sep–Nov rule does as well or better (+Rs 5,508/t).

Constant loss rates

ICAR 120-day trials extrapolated to 8 months; monsoon sprouting may raise losses. At 8% a month the cold store still gains.

Not modelled

Quality grading, credit beyond L, export bans (2019, 2020, 2023). Price impact is small: the FPO is 1.8–3.6% of arrivals.

Small sample

8 seasons, 2 of them test seasons: the tests are indicative. 2018 was a losing year for cold storage (−Rs 1,181/t).
